## Rules

- Never quote a number you cannot back. Where a file shows `[your number]`, replace it with your real figure.
- Say "I" for what you did and "we" for what the team did.
- Say "I don't know" fast, then reason out loud.
- Keep project descriptions generic when speaking publicly: no customer or company secrets.
