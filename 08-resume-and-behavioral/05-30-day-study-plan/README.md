# 30-Day Study Plan

[← Section index](../README.md) · Prev: [Rapid-Fire Question Bank](../04-rapid-fire-question-bank/README.md)

Assumes about 1.5 to 2 hours a day. Topics are ordered by weight: highest first. Folder numbers are section/topic, e.g. 02/09 is the worker pool. Weights are shares of total prep effort and are estimates, not measured data.

## In one line
Study the highest-weight topics first, then fill in the rest, and keep the last days for mocks and your own stories.

## Daily routine
1. Read the README (10 min).
2. If the topic has a `main.go`, run and read it (10 min).
3. Answer the Q&A out loud without looking (10 min).
4. For DSA days, solve one problem from scratch (30 min).

Unlisted topics are lower weight (e.g. 01/11 generics, 02/06, 05/08, 06/07, 07/11): cover them in days 26-27 after your weak spots, never before the top ones.
